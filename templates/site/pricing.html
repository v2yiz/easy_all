Frequently asked

What counts as a modelled source?

A schema, connector or event stream that feeds at least one published model. Staging copies and sandboxes are free.

Do you store a copy of our data?

No. Model SQL runs in your warehouse. Only metadata, model definitions and query results cached for dashboards are stored.

Can we deploy inside our own cloud?

Yes, on Enterprise. The deployment is one container plus a read-only warehouse role, and it can run air-gapped after install.

How does annual billing work?

Annual plans are invoiced up front with a 14-day trial that converts only if you keep the workspace. We do not auto-charge during trials.

What happens if we add a source mid-term?

Additional sources are prorated at the current plan rate and can be removed at renewal.

Not sure which plan fits?

Send us your warehouse, team size and the questions you want to answer. We will map it to a plan in one call.
