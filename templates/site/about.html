Company

We build the layer between raw events and decisions

Northlake started in 2019 after two data leads compared their activation dashboards and found they disagreed by 23%.

Why we exist

Most companies do not have a data problem. They have a definition problem: the same metric computed five ways, each with a different owner and no test.

Northlake makes the definition the artifact. It lives in git, has an owner, runs tests on every change and is the only thing dashboards are allowed to read.

We are 38 people across three offices, remote-first since 2021, and we still answer support tickets ourselves.

What we optimise for

Three commitments that shape the roadmap more than any customer request.

Boring reliability

A dashboard that is down during the board meeting is worse than no dashboard. We publish uptime, incidents and postmortems.

No data copies

Your warehouse stays the source of truth. We would rather lose a feature than duplicate regulated data.

Readable by default

Every model has a plain-language description. If a new hire cannot explain a metric, the definition is not finished.
